## Day 4
### LinkedIn
```
I see people say it all the time: "n8n is only good for demos, you can't run production on it".
This is the biggest myth in automation right now.

I currently have 13 production client systems running on n8n. Some of them process over 5000 requests per day. One of them has been running uninterrupted for 8 months without a single second of downtime.
The difference between a demo and a production n8n system is only three things:
1.  Proper error handling: retries with backoff, dead letter queues, and instant alerts when something fails instead of silent failures.
2.  Proper logging and observability: you can trace every single execution end to end, see exactly what step failed and why.
3.  Staging environment testing: every change gets tested in staging before it touches production.

If you add those three things, n8n is more reliable than a lot of custom code systems I've seen shipped by expensive engineering teams.
Most people build a 10 minute demo, ship it to production with none of those three things, it breaks after a week, and then they say n8n isn't ready for production.

Have you ever had an n8n workflow fail in production? What broke?

#n8n #ProductionAutomation #WorkflowAutomation
```
